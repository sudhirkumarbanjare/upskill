# Privacy Policy for 5Upskill Platform

**Last Updated**: September 20, 2026

5Upskill ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **5Upskill: Learn & Earn Projects** (Student App), **5Upskill for Clients** (Client App), and **5Upskill Admin Console** (Admin App). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile applications and related services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Data**: Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and Role (Student, Corporate Client, Administrator).
- **Profile Information**: Educational qualifications, skills, portfolio links, company name, GST/corporate registration number (for clients).
- **Authentication Credentials**: SMS OTP authentication tokens processed securely via Firebase Phone Authentication.

### B. Project & Marketplace Data
- **Project Listings & Proposals**: Descriptions, deliverables, milestones, budgets, and submission attachments.
- **Masterclass Progress & Assessments**: Quiz answers, assessment scores, completed lessons, and certificates issued.

### C. Device & Telemetry Data
- **Device Information**: Android OS version, device model, and unique push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Network & Diagnostics**: Connection status, error crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics), and app performance metrics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use collected data to:
1. Authenticate your identity and provide role-specific access (Student / Client / Admin).
2. Facilitate matching between corporate clients and verified student talent.
3. Track and evaluate skill assessments and masterclass curriculum progress.
4. Process milestone escrow payments and disbursements.
5. Send transactional push notifications, project updates, and security alerts.
6. Enforce platform terms of service, fraud prevention, and corporate partner compliance.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services
We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with trusted service providers strictly necessary for operating our platform:
- **Google Cloud Platform & Firebase**: Cloud Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Messaging (FCM).
- **Google Play Services**: Play Integrity API for app security and anti-tampering verification.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage
- All network communications are encrypted in transit using **TLS 1.3 / HTTPS**.
- Sensitive data is stored securely in **Google Cloud Platform / Cloud Firestore** data centers with multi-layer role-based security rules.
- Financial transactions use end-to-end tokenized gateways without storing sensitive card credentials on our servers.

---

## 5. Account & Data Deletion
Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.
- **In-App**: Navigate to **Profile** -> **Account Settings** -> **Delete Account**.
- **Web Form**: Submit a deletion request at `https://5upskill.com/delete-account` or email `privacy@5upskill.com`.
- Upon verification, all personal profile records will be permanently purged within 30 days, except records required to be retained for statutory tax/financial compliance.
